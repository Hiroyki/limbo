# Privacy policy for Limbo

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Limbo collects no data. It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else, and it contains no analytics, ads or remote code.

## Erasing

- **History.** Limbo asks Chrome to erase browsing data. It does not request permission to read your history.
- **Other data.** Cache, the download list, and cookies with site data are erased only if you tick them in settings.
- **The current tab.** When you click the icon, Chrome gives Limbo temporary access to the tab you are on. Limbo uses it only to draw its animation there. It does not read the page.
- **Google's pages.** Limbo can open Google's My Activity pages for you. What you do there is between you and Google.

## Limbo Lock

- **Passcode and recovery code.** Stored on your computer only as salted hashes (PBKDF2 with SHA-256, 600,000 rounds). The originals are not kept.
- **Windows.** Limbo minimizes and restores Chrome windows.
- **Locked tabs.** The address of a locked tab is kept in memory until it is unlocked or Chrome closes.
- **Locked sites.** For each site you lock, Chrome asks you to give Limbo access to that site. Limbo uses it only to notice when the site is opened. The list of sites is stored on your computer.
- **Idle time.** If you turn on automatic locking, Chrome tells Limbo when the computer has been idle. This is not recorded.

## What is stored

Your settings, how many times you have used Limbo, the list of locked sites, and the hashes above. They stay on your computer, and uninstalling Limbo deletes them.

## Contact

Use the support tab on Limbo's Chrome Web Store page.
